The project has a new name: Omamobile.

We're renaming it to make clear that it is an independent community project. It still runs Omarchy on your phone, and nothing about that changes.

If you have installed it, there is nothing to do. Your phone keeps its packages, its updates and its settings, and `omarchy update` works as before.

The website stays at [omamobile.org](https://omamobile.org), and this account stays the same. The GitHub repositories will move to the new name later; the old links will keep working.

Omamobile is an independent community project built on [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org). It is not an official Omarchy project.
